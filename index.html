How to Survive

  • Move & Peek: Mouse to look, WASD to move, Space to jump, Shift to crouch silently, Q/Z to lean around corners, F for flashlight.
  • Hiding Spots: Press E near wardrobes, desks, or crates upstairs to hide. If an NPC sees you duck in, they'll confront you immediately (+20% Curiosity).
  • Light & Sound: Your flashlight flickers when an NPC is close. Walls dampen sound, but creaky boards and grabbing artifacts alert nearby NPCs.
  • Lockdown Escape: Steal all 5 artifacts upstairs, then juke the ambush downstairs to reach the Main Door.
