The Woods House — Prototype

Games 1–4: NPCs run on deterministic C++ WebAssembly rules with 3D AABB raycast line-of-sight.

Game 5+: A 9-feature PyTorch Neural Network (ONNX) activates in your browser to predict mood shifts from stealth, missing items, and floor checks, while C++ Euclidean Distance selects choice-aware dialogue.

How to Survive

  • Move & Stealth: Mouse to look, WASD to move, Space to jump, Shift to crouch silently (shrinks NPC detection range), F to toggle flashlight.
  • Light & Sound: Shining your flashlight near NPCs or stepping on creaky floorboards upstairs draws them toward you.
  • Floor Checks & Trust: NPCs periodically check downstairs—if you're missing, their Curiosity spikes. High Trust lets you talk your way out; low Trust makes them aggressive.
  • Lockdown Escape: Steal all 3 artifacts upstairs, then sprint downstairs to the Main Door before they catch you.
